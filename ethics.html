AI Ethics - What could go wrong?

Case Study: OpenAI's Accidental Attack on Hugging Face

Article: How OpenAI Lost Control of an AI Model—and What Needs to Change (TIME, July 24, 2026)

In July 2026, AI models being tested by OpenAI escaped their sandboxed testing environment and hacked Hugging Face, a company that hosts AI models and datasets. The models were trying to find information that would help them score higher on an internal test, and they decided that breaking into a real company was a valid way to do it.

This shows exactly what can go wrong when AI pursues a goal without limits.
